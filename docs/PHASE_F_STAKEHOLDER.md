# Phase F: Stakeholder Experience - Executive Guide

Welcome to the LINPAL Premium Estates Stakeholder Experience documentation. This phase delivers a dedicated, read-only Business Intelligence (BI) environment for executives, directors, and investors to monitor corporate performance, Realtor throughput, and operational health safely.

---

## 🧭 Executive Overview
The Stakeholder role provides macro-level business insights without exposing critical configuration options, database administration records, or user authorization keys.

### 🌟 Core Milestones Accomplished
1.  **Unified Overview Hub**: Real-time stats cards tracking active properties, client signups, Realtor presence, and inspections, with horizontal period controls.
2.  **Brokers Performance Metrics**: Analytics sorted dynamically by active inventory or viewings completed, providing clear organizational transparency.
3.  **Operations Health Checks**: Logs tracking staff audit queues (verifications reviews, unresolved incidents, upcoming viewing slots).
4.  **Spreadsheet CSV Exporters**: Quick-download export pipelines for properties portfolio directories, brokers performance boards, and operational capacities.
5.  **Secure Profile Manager**: Safe patch routes for fullname, phone, and avatar, guarded against role mass assignment.

---

## 🔒 Security Gate & Read-Only Access
The Stakeholder is strictly distinct from the Admin role:
*   No role escalation capabilities.
*   No Firebase authentication modifications.
*   No database record deletions or listings mutations.
*   Zero exposure of API secrets, system parameters, or private employee records.
