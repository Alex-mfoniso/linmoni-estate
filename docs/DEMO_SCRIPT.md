# Demo Script

Target duration: 5 to 8 minutes.

## Flow

1. Sign in as a client.
2. Browse and filter properties.
3. Open a property detail page and review the gallery.
4. Save a favorite property.
5. Book an inspection.
6. Open messages and show the conversation history.
7. Show the notification badge and recent alerts.
8. Sign out and sign in as a realtor.
9. Open My Properties and view property management.
10. Open Bookings and approve or complete a request.
11. Sign out and sign in as an admin.
12. Open Users and show direct account creation.
13. Open Invitations and show invite management.
14. Open Properties and show platform-wide moderation.
15. Open Analytics and highlight the summary cards.

## Demo Tips

- Keep the pace brisk and focus on business value.
- Use stable demo data and avoid live edits that are hard to undo.
- If a section is empty, switch to seeded demo data before the presentation.
