## 🧭 Step-by-Step Walkthrough Flow

### Step 1: Browse & Inquire (Logged in as Client)
1. **Login**: Enter `client1@linpal.demo` and `password123`.
2. **Overview**: View the sleek, editorial landing feed displaying featured waterfront villas and listings.
3. **Property Detail**: Tap `Luxury Waterfront Apartments`. Show the beautiful image galleries, pricing blocks, and amenities badges.
4. **Action**: Tap **Request Inspection**, select an upcoming date slot, enter a description, and submit.
5. **Messaging**: Tap the **Chat** bubble on the top right, navigate to messages, and send: *"Hi, looking forward to viewing the apartment next week!"*

### Step 2: Manage & Respond (Logged in as Realtor)
1. **Login**: Switch account to `realtor1@linpal.demo` / `password123`.
2. **Inbox**: Open the incoming chat from `client1@linpal.demo`, and respond: *"Hi! The scheduled inspection is confirmed. See you next week!"*
3. **Leads / Booking**: Navigate to the **Bookings** tab to see the confirmed appointment.

### Step 3: Audit & Approve (Logged in as Staff)
1. **Login**: Switch account to `staff1@linpal.demo` / `password123`.
2. **Verification**: Review incoming properties submissions in the verification queue.
3. **Approve**: Confirm listing details and tap **Approve & Publish**.

### Step 4: Analyze Portfolio Performance (Logged in as Stakeholder / Investor)
1. **Login**: Switch account to `stakeholder@linpal.demo` / `password123`.
2. **Dashboard**: Present the analytical KPIs (Active listings, new clients, completed inspections).
3. **Charts**: Review the distribution metrics visual charts.
4. **Reports**: Tap **Generate Properties Report** to download the ready CSV summary.

### Step 5: Oversee Platform Settings & Audits (Logged in as Admin)
1. **Login**: Switch account to `admin@linpal.demo` / `password123`.
2. **Console**: Present User lists, tap a user profile to demonstrate secure role transitions or suspensions.
3. **Settings**: Show the global switches for Maintenance Mode, Listings Approval requirements, and the permitted amenities tag builder.
4. **Audit Logs**: Show the immutable, color-coded security audit logs showing who changed what on the platform.
